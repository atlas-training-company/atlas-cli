# Training frequency and splits

Evidence: The dose-response meta-regression found a clearer positive frequency relationship for strength than for hypertrophy; the hypertrophy estimates were compatible with negligible effects. This does not establish an optimal number of days for everyone. [pelland-2026-dose-response]

Practice inference: Choose frequency around schedule, recovery, and the ability to perform quality weekly sets. Spread work over another session when long sessions degrade execution or make the plan hard to complete. Redistributing existing sets and adding new sets are different changes and should be recorded separately.

Applicability and limits: Findings summarize varied adult resistance-training studies rather than directly comparing every split. More frequent practice may serve a specific strength skill, while hypertrophy programming can use several viable schedules. Evaluate a rolling training period when a rotation crosses calendar weeks; do not add make-up work solely to satisfy a calendar label.
