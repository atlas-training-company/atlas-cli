# Load and repetition ranges

Evidence: Across a large network of adult trials, resistance-training prescriptions improved muscle size and strength compared with no exercise. Higher-load conditions favored maximal strength, while muscle growth was broadly similar across the tested prescriptions. [currier-2023-prescription]

Practice inference: Use heavier work when the goal includes expressing strength against heavy loads. For hypertrophy, select a repetition range that permits controlled execution and adequate effort without discomfort or breathlessness becoming the main limit. Moderate repetitions are a convenient default, not a uniquely anabolic zone.

Applicability and limits: The review classified load in broad categories and combined sets and frequency; it does not prove that every possible rep range works equally well. Changing load can change technique and fatigue. Keep the chosen range stable enough to judge progress, and avoid translating a population-level ranking into mandatory percentages for an individual.
