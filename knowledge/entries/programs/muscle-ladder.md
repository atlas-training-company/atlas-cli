# The Muscle Ladder framework

Framework: The supplied summaries of The Muscle Ladder emphasize sustainability and the basic training decisions before advanced methods. Consistent execution, suitable exercises, effort, and progression are useful first places to investigate a stalled plan. This is an editorial coaching framework, not a tested ranking of causal importance. [nippard-2024-muscle-ladder]

Practice application: Match a program's demands to actual experience, available days, equipment, and recovery. A more advanced label or more elaborate split does not by itself make a plan appropriate. Use a simpler, repeatable option when complexity prevents consistent training.

Source and scope: This entry draws on supplied summarized notes; original pages and full program tables were not checked. It does not reproduce the book's programs or establish exact set, repetition, or effort recommendations.
