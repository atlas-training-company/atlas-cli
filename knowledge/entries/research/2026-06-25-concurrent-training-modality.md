## Generalized summary

The imported note reports the following interpretation:

Concurrent-training modality comparisons are limited by small datasets and heterogeneity. Whole-muscle, fiber-level, and strength outcomes can differ; a ranking among running, cycling, and other modalities should not be treated as a universal interference hierarchy.
